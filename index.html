How Fund Nexus Works

A transparent four-step cycle ensuring complete visibility from the moment a cause is created to the exact rupee of reported expenditure.

1

Cause Registration

Fundraisers submit causes with specific financial goals, detailed purposes, and required milestone projections.

2

Simulated Giving

Donors contribute to campaigns through simulated donations, receiving instant transaction receipts and tracking tokens.

3

Transparent Ledger

Transactions are instantly recorded into the immutable fund ledger, calculating running balances in real-time.

4

Admin & Evidence Audit

Disbursements require proof of purchase. Only administrator-verified expenses are deducted from the verified balance.
